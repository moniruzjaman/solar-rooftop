Executive Summary

1.1 Purpose. This proposal seeks administrative and financial approval for installation of a 25.3 kWp grid-connected rooftop solar system at the office building to offset approximately 78% of daytime electricity consumption, reduce exposure to rising commercial tariffs (৳10.55/kWh), and fulfill the organization's renewable energy obligations under the Renewable Energy Policy of Bangladesh.

1.2 Procurement Philosophy. Rather than importing a complete turnkey system, the proposal adopts a "Import Core, Build Local" approach: only non-manufacturable core components (solar modules/inverter) are imported, while mounting structure, cabling, switchgear, earthing and civil works are sourced from Bangladeshi industry. This ensures any local electrician or workshop can maintain the system without import lead-time or foreign vendor dependence.

1.3 Financial Justification. Against a one-time investment of ৳16.48 lakh, the system will save approximately ৳3.65 lakh annually, achieving payback in 4.5 years and generating net benefit of ~৳74.8 lakh over its 25-year certified life. Financing via IDCOL green loan (6–8% interest) preserves organizational capital.

Procurement Strategy — Import Core Only, Build & Maintain Locally

To protect foreign exchange and guarantee that the system can be maintained by local technicians with minimum effort, the complete system shall not be imported turnkey. Only components with no domestic manufacturing capability shall be imported; all structural and electrical balance shall be fabricated from Bangladeshi raw materials and industry.

MUST IMPORT (No Local Alternative)

≈ ৳10.13 L • 61%
1. Tier-1 Solar Modules (or M10 mono cells for local lamination)

Cell wafer fabrication exists only in CN/DE/MY. Local assemblers (Rahimafrooz, GrameenSun) themselves import cells — importing cells/modules directly is equivalent and warranty-backed.

2. Inverter Power Core (IGBT modules, MPPT control PCB)

Semiconductor power stages not manufactured domestically. Import as sealed unit with 10-yr warranty — replacement is a 30-minute swap, not a repair.

3. TÜV-Certified MC4 Connectors & Bypass Diodes

Certified connector metallurgy (Stäubli-grade) unavailable locally. Standardization means any future panel swaps plug in without rewiring.

4. Bi-directional Net Meter + Smart Logger (if not DISCO-supplied)

Class-1 certified revenue-grade metering per Net Metering Guidelines 2025.

5. LiFePO₄ Grade-A Cells — only if Option C approved

CATL/EVE prismatic cells; local BMS assembly and enclosure fabrication possible.

SOURCE LOCALLY (BD Raw Materials)

≈ ৳6.35 L • 39%
1. Mounting Structure — GI Steel (BSRM/KSRM/GPH sections)

Hot-dip galvanized angle/channel fabricated by local workshops. Any welder in Bangladesh can repair it — zero import dependency.

2. DC & AC Cables — BRB / Polycable / EnergyPac

Solar-grade UV cable and XLPE armored cable from domestic manufacturers; replacement stock available at any electrical market nationwide.

3. DCDB/ACDB Enclosures, SPD, Breakers — local sheet-metal + assembly

IP65 enclosures fabricated locally; Schneider/ABB/Havells breakers assembled in-country — every part stocked by local dealers.

4. Earthing & Lightning Protection — GI pipe, copper strip

Two earth pits with locally available GI electrodes, copper conductor, charcoal/salt treatment.

5. Civil Works, SS-304 Fasteners, Labor & Commissioning

Roof waterproofing pads, walkways, local skilled labor — creates domestic employment and keeps O&M knowledge inside the country.

Cost Split: Import vs Local

Why This Split Minimizes Maintenance Effort

Repair, don't import: structure & wiring repairable by any local workshop/electrician — no foreign lead time.
Swap, don't fix: imported items are modular (panel/inverter); failure = 30-min plug-and-play replacement under warranty.
Standard parts: MC4, IEC 61215/61730 standards mean no proprietary components anywhere in the system.
Spares in-country: 2% spare panels + 1 string fuse set stored on site; cables/breakers off-the-shelf in Dhaka.
Knowledge retention: local installation trains our own staff; O&M never depends on a foreign vendor.
Forex economy: 39% of spend stays in Bangladeshi industry; aligned with Renewable Energy Policy & local-content goals.

Low-Effort Maintenance Plan (25-Year Horizon)

Remote Monitoring (Daily)

Huawei FusionSolar app auto-alerts on any string fault. Zero physical inspection needed — office admin reviews mobile dashboard 2 minutes/week.

Cleaning (Seasonal)

Every 2 weeks Oct–Mar (dry/dusty season). Zero cleaning Jun–Sep — monsoon rains wash panels naturally. Plain water + soft brush; no chemicals.

Inspection (Annual)

One pre-monsoon visit: bolt torque check, thermal imaging of connections, earth resistance test. Performed by local EPC at ~৳8,000/yr.
